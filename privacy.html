Deep Mine is a game made by BlueGill Innovations Inc. ("we" or "us"). This page explains what information the game uses and why. If you have a question, email us at deepmineappsupport@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Deep Mine doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
  • Your progress, crystals and settings are saved on your phone, and they stay there.
  • If we ever add something that uses your information, such as ads or purchases, we will update this page and tell you in the game first.

What the game saves on your phone

  • Your progress: coins, crystals, tools, upgrades, the sites you have reached, the state of your mine, your daily reward streak, missions and trophies.
  • Your settings: sound, music, vibration, battery saver and left-handed controls.
  • How you dressed your miner.
  • A random player code, shown in Settings under About. It is made of random letters and numbers, it is not linked to you or to your phone, and it only helps us find a problem if you write to us.

All of this stays on your phone. We can't see it, and it is never sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it. Reset progress in Settings starts the mines over, and keeps your crystals, skins and trophies.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't let anyone track you.
  • We don't sell or share any information about you.

When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and we delete it within 12 months after we last hear from you.

Children

Deep Mine doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included. If a child emails us, we use the email only to answer, and then delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct or delete it. The only information we could ever hold is an email you sent us. To ask about it, email deepmineappsupport@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page. If the change matters, for example because the game starts using information it didn't before, we will also tell you in the game before it happens.
